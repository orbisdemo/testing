The Challenge

Enterprise transformation doesn't fail because of bad ideas. It fails because decisions become fragmented.

Business demands are captured in one tool. Budgets are managed in another. Procurement follows a separate workflow. Project execution happens elsewhere. Engineering teams work in completely different environments.

By the time leadership reviews progress, the original business intent has been diluted, timelines have shifted, and decisions are based on incomplete information.

The Five Decision Lens Framework

Every decision deserves more than an opinion.

Orbis replaces subjective judgement with measurable intelligence. Every approval, investment, milestone, vendor evaluation, and project decision is assessed consistently across five dimensions.

01

Confidence

How certain is this decision?

Measures data completeness, stakeholder alignment, and historical accuracy.

02

Risk

What could go wrong?

Continuously evaluates delivery complexity, dependencies, and strategic exposure.

03

Financial Exposure

What is the cost at risk?

Models cost of delay, scope creep, and resource variance before commitments are made.

04

Delivery Readiness

Can we actually deliver this?

Validates skills, resources, timelines, and organisational capacity.

05

Accountability

Who owns this outcome?

Assigns ownership with a complete audit trail from approval through delivery.
